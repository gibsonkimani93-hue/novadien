Helpful information

Questions, answered clearly.

As Novadien develops, this section will help customers understand the service, product availability, and order process.

Can I order Pregnich or Osteodien right now?

These products are shown as catalogue placeholders. Their exact identity, approved product information, availability, and applicable supply requirements must be verified before orders can be accepted.

Are prices and stock levels live?

No. This prototype can demonstrate catalogue and management workflows, but prices and inventory should not be treated as live until connected to verified operational records.

How will prescription medicines be handled?

Any medicine requiring a prescription must go through the appropriate prescription and regulatory checks before it can be supplied. The website must not bypass those checks.

How will delivery tracking work?

The system records order status and can show the delivery worker’s real GPS updates when the supplier starts sharing location. Automatic payments and external alerts still require separate integrations.

How can a supplier work with Novadien?

Suppliers can register their business details for review. They must be appropriately verified and authorised before being approved to supply or deliver medicines.
